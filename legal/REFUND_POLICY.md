## EU and UK customers: right of withdrawal

If you are a consumer in the EU or UK, you have a legal right to withdraw from an online purchase within 14 days.

Deploy King Cloud is a digital service that starts as soon as you subscribe. At checkout we ask you to confirm that you want the service to start immediately. Where the law allows, you then lose the right of withdrawal for the part of the service already provided. Our [7]-day full refund on first purchases still applies.

## How refunds are paid

- Refunds go back to the original payment method (card, UPI or bank account), through the payment provider that took the payment.
- We start the refund within **5 working days** of approving it. Your bank may take a further 5–10 working days to show it.
- Refunds include the taxes that were charged.
- Where Paddle sold you the plan as merchant of record, the refund is made by Paddle under this policy.
