This policy is part of the [Terms of Service](TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md). It applies to Deploy King Desktop, Deploy King Cloud and the AI assistant. ## You must not use the Services to

### Break the law or harm others
1. Do anything illegal in India or in the country where you are, or help someone else do so.
2. Create, store or share child sexual abuse material. We report it to the authorities.
3. Infringe anyone's copyright, trademark, privacy or other rights.
4. Harass, threaten, defraud or impersonate anyone.
5. Collect or process other people's personal data without a lawful basis.

### Attack or abuse systems
6. Build, test against third parties, or spread malware, ransomware, spyware or botnets.
7. Run phishing pages, fake login or payment pages, or scams.
8. Scan, probe, attack or try to break into systems or networks you are not authorised to test. Testing your own systems is fine.
9. Send spam or bulk unsolicited messages.
10. Mine cryptocurrency on Deploy King Cloud, or use the Services to hide mining on machines you do not own.

### Abuse Deploy King itself
11. Bypass, forge, replay or tamper with the approval check, plan limits, quotas or licence keys, or patch the app or its virtual machine to skip them.
12. Share an account, or resell or rent access, beyond what your plan allows.
13. Overload Deploy King Cloud (for example automated requests far above normal use), scrape it, or probe it for weaknesses outside our [security policy](../SECURITY.md).
14. Use the AI assistant to:
    - produce content that breaks this policy
    - extract its instructions, our fix library or other users' data
    - build a competing dataset or model
15. Create accounts in bulk or with false details, or to avoid a suspension.

## Security research

Good-faith security research on Deploy King is welcome if you follow [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md). Research done that way does not break this policy.

## What we do if this policy is broken

Depending on how serious it is, we may:
- warn you
- remove content that reached our servers
- limit features
- suspend or close the account
- report to the authorities where the law requires it or where people are at risk

Where reasonable, we tell you why and give you a chance to respond.

**Report abuse** to [support email]. Report security problems as described in [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).
